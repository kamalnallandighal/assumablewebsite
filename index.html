The basics

Questions everyone asks

Do I need to be a veteran to assume a VA loan?

No. VA loans can be assumed by any qualified buyer — military status is not a requirement. This is the #1 misconception about VA assumptions.

How much do I need for a down payment?

You need to cover the seller's equity — typically 8–15% of the home's value, depending on how long they've owned it. Not 20%.

Is the rate really locked for me?

Yes. You take over their existing loan, so you inherit their rate and term. If they locked at 2.75% for 30 years in 2020, you continue at 2.75% for the remaining 25 years.

What does Jeff charge?

Jeff is paid by the seller as the listing or buyer's agent, the same as any standard transaction. There's no extra fee for using Assumable.
